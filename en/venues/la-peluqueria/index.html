Meet La Peluquería 2.0

04

La Peluquería 2.0 is our cocktail bar at Calle de Alberto Alcocer, 32, in Chamartín. A place to let your hair down without heading into the centre: drinks, music and people who want the weekend to start on Thursday.

Open Thursday to Saturday from 8pm to 2am. If you live or work around the northern end of the Castellana, this is your spot for a drink after work.

  • Drinks in the north of the city, no need to go downtown
  • Thursday and Friday after-work
  • Birthdays and group celebrations

FAQ

Quick questions

What are the opening hours of La Peluquería 2.0?

La Peluquería 2.0 is open Thursday to Saturday 20:00–02:00. Closed the rest of the week.

Where is La Peluquería 2.0?

At Calle de Alberto Alcocer, 32, Madrid (Chamartín).

Can I book a table at La Peluquería 2.0?

Yes, message us on WhatsApp at +34 656 48 50 99 and we’ll save you a spot. For large groups, give us a few days’ notice.

Can I celebrate a birthday or a private event at La Peluquería 2.0?

Of course. We host birthdays, company dinners, hen and stag parties and celebrations. Tell us the date and how many of you there are on WhatsApp or at hola@grupodospuntocero.com.
