FAQ

Quick questions

How do I book an event with Grupo 2.0?

Book online with the “Book an event online” button, message us on WhatsApp at +34 656 48 50 99 or email hola@grupodospuntocero.com with the date, the number of people and the kind of plan. You can write to us in English.

Do you have set menus for company dinners?

Yes. La Traviesa 2.0 has Christmas group menus from €45 to €65 per person with drinks included, plus a cocktail-style option for large groups. They’re at grupodospuntocero.com/traviesa (in Spanish). For other dates we put together a tailored proposal.

How far in advance should I book?

The sooner the better, especially in December and on Fridays and Saturdays, when the venues fill up most. Get in touch even if you don’t know the exact headcount yet.

Can we have dinner and keep partying in the same place?

Yes: at Green 2.0 (Paseo de la Castellana, 40 bis) you can have dinner and stay on in the nightclub until 6am, Thursday to Saturday.

Can I book out a whole venue?

It depends on the venue, the date and the size of your group. Tell us your plan and we’ll let you know the options.
